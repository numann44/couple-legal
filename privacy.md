# Couple Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Couple is developed by Ahmet Numan Şahin. Contact: **numanshn44@gmail.com**. This policy describes the current Couple service and iOS application. Optional features have separate choices; connecting with someone does not give them control of your phone.

## Your account and profile

You can sign in with Apple, Google or verified email. We store an account identifier, the identifiers returned by your chosen sign-in provider, available email information, and session records. Email passwords are stored as password hashes, not plain text. Apple may provide a private relay email address. Connecting additional sign-in methods requires verification; matching email addresses do not automatically merge accounts.

Your profile stores your chosen name, birthday and avatar selections. Your connected partner sees your name and avatar, not your birthday, sign-in tokens or account email. The service is for adults aged 18 and over; onboarding accepts ages 18–100. We do not knowingly provide the service to children. Contact us if you believe a child’s information was submitted.

## Diaries, quizzes, photos and wallpaper

New signed-in content is saved to your account. Preview and older device-only content are kept separately and are not uploaded automatically.

- Diaries start as **Private**. Choosing **For us** shares the entry and its attached photos with that relationship. Your partner cannot edit your entry.
- Quiz answers remain hidden from the other participant until both finish the round. Completed rounds show comparisons and matching answers; this is an activity, not a psychological assessment.
- Photos you explicitly upload are stored privately and retrieved through authenticated access. The server reprocesses photos and removes embedded location/EXIF metadata. The picture itself may still reveal personal information.
- A wallpaper proposal shares the selected image with your current partner so they can review it. Approval records a shared choice. Each person must save and apply it manually in iOS; Couple never remotely changes phone wallpaper.

After unpairing, previously shared diaries, photos and completed quizzes remain readable to the former participants, unless their owner deletes the content or withdraws access by changing supported privacy settings. A new partner cannot access that archive. Current wallpaper access and unfinished quiz rounds end with the relationship. Photos or screenshots another person already saved outside Couple cannot be remotely erased.

## Optional location and phone status

Location sharing is off by default and requires a verified partner connection, your explicit sharing choice and iOS permission. We store the latest valid location, its accuracy and update time for this feature. Coordinates may be precise or approximate depending on your permission. Location more than 120 seconds old is not shown as current; distance is available only when both required locations are valid.

Turning sharing off stops collection on that device and revokes server sharing when the request is confirmed. If you are offline, the app states that server confirmation is pending; a previously received current location expires under the 120-second freshness rule. Background sharing requires an additional choice and appropriate iOS permission, and is not guaranteed to update continuously. Unpairing and account deletion revoke partner location access.

Phone status has its own sharing preference. When enabled, battery, charging and connection information may be sent to your current partner; updates over five minutes old are marked stale. This is not a screen-use history. Route history and place alerts require verified Pro access and separate choices. With your consent, your route points and reported visits are retained for up to seven days. You may choose up to five named places for your own location; arrival/departure notifications do not include exact addresses. Turning location sharing off removes your partner's access to your location history. Your own retained history can be deleted and otherwise expires after seven days. Locked features do not collect a route history.

## Widgets, Screen Time and our cat

Widget design presets and supported preferences are stored on your signed-in account. Widgets on your own device receive only the summaries needed for their selected design, such as names, avatar images, relationship date, shared feeling, selected photo, battery summary and currently valid distance. Exact coordinates, birthdays, private diary entries and session tokens are not written to the widget shared data area.

Personal Screen Time is not available in this distributed release. A separate development-only report uses Apple’s individual authorization and Device Activity report extension on a supported physical device. That report stays on the device: usage measurements are not sent to Couple’s server, a partner or the widget data area. Other widgets do not require Screen Time permission.

If you adopt the shared cat, its name, feeding/petting times and resting state are stored for your active relationship. A new relationship does not inherit a previous relationship’s cat.

## Permissions and notifications

The system photo picker grants access to selected images rather than your entire library. Saving a wallpaper to Photos is an explicit action. Camera access is used to scan a pairing QR code; camera frames are not saved or uploaded by that scanner. Notifications are optional. APNs device tokens and a delivery queue support account updates; push messages use generic descriptions rather than diary text, answers, photos or exact addresses. Data synchronization does not depend on granting notification permission.

## Service providers and purposes

Couple Pro uses Apple in-app purchases and RevenueCat to validate and manage subscription access. RevenueCat receives an opaque account UUID, purchase/subscription information and the technical information required by its SDK. It does not receive your birthday, diary text, private photos, quiz answers or precise coordinates from Couple. We do not send your email as the subscription identifier. Each account has its own identifier; our server checks the current accepted partner connection to extend one subscription to both people, without merging their accounts. Ending that connection removes the former partner's inherited benefit. Apple handles payment details; Couple does not receive your card number. You can restore purchases and manage renewal through Apple. Privacy controls, unpairing and account deletion do not require Pro.

We process information to create and secure accounts, provide the features you choose, synchronize account settings, handle support, and prevent abuse. Optional partner sharing is based on your choices; consent can be withdrawn. Where applicable, processing also relies on providing the requested service and legitimate interests in security and reliability, subject to your legal rights.

Service providers include Oracle Cloud for hosting, Cloudflare for domain/DNS services, Brevo for account emails, Apple for sign-in, APNs, maps and system features, and Google when you choose Google sign-in. Public policy/support documents are hosted on GitHub. These providers handle information needed for their services under their own policies. They may process data in countries other than your own. We do not sell your personal information, run advertising in this build, or use Couple information for cross-app advertising tracking.

## Security and retention

API communication uses HTTPS. Access is checked against the signed-in account and, where relevant, the relationship and sharing choice. Media is not served through a public photo gallery. Session tokens are protected using iOS Keychain. No system can promise absolute security.

Account information and content are retained while needed for your account and requested features, unless you delete them. Current-location visibility expires after 120 seconds; freshness is not a promise that every database record or backup is erased at that exact moment. Pending uploads not attached to content are eligible for cleanup after 24 hours. Queued notifications and security records may be kept as needed for delivery, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

Deleting your account ends your sessions and relationship access and removes your account-owned content from the active service, without deleting the other person’s own content. Restricted backup copies may remain until those backups are removed; backups are not available to partners or used as normal application data. Contact support for a retention or deletion request, including backup concerns. We do not promise a fixed backup-erasure period that is not currently enforced.

## Your choices and rights

Use **Us → Account & Relationship → Connected accounts** to manage sign-in methods and access account deletion, which requires reauthentication. You can stop sharing, unpair, delete supported content and manage iOS permissions. Deleting only local preview data or uninstalling the app does not delete your server account.

For access, correction, export, deletion, objections or other applicable privacy rights, email **numanshn44@gmail.com**. We may need to verify that a request belongs to the account owner without asking for a password or verification code. Where applicable, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when practices change. Material changes involving new collection or sharing will be explained in the application before the relevant choice is enabled.

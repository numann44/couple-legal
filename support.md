## Contact us

Include the app version, iOS version, the screen where the problem happens and a short description. A screenshot is optional: hide names, emails, photos, precise locations and other private information first. Never send your password, verification code, session token or private key. There is no guaranteed response time or emergency support service.

## Account and privacy

Manage sign-in methods and account deletion in **Us → Account & Relationship → Connected accounts**. Deletion requires verifying your identity. Uninstalling the app or deleting device-only preview data does not delete your server account. If you cannot sign in, contact support for a verified account request.

Disconnecting a partner stops current relationship sharing. Earlier shared diaries, photos and completed quizzes remain in the former participants’ archive unless the content owner deletes them or changes supported privacy settings. Your new partner cannot see those archives. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).

## Add a widget

Save a design in **Love Bag → Widget**. Then touch and hold the iPhone Home Screen, choose **Edit → Add Widget → Couple**, select a size and add it. Touch and hold the widget, choose **Edit Widget** and select your saved design. Saving in Couple does not automatically add a Home Screen widget. iOS decides when widgets refresh.

## Shared wallpaper

Choose an image in **Love Bag → Wallpaper**, send it for approval and have your partner review it there. After approval, use **Save or share wallpaper → Save Image**. Each person then opens **Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photos**. Couple cannot automatically change either phone’s system wallpaper. Notification delivery depends on permission and connectivity.

## Location and phone status

In **Distance**, location sharing and phone status are separate choices. A partner connection and iOS location permission are required for location sharing. Approximate permission gives approximate information. When location is unavailable or stale, Couple does not invent a position or distance. Background updates can be delayed by iOS. Offline stopping is marked confirmation pending until the server confirms it.

## Personal Screen Time

The personal report requires Apple authorization and a supported physical device; the simulator does not provide a real usage report. Usage stays in the on-device report and is not sent to your partner or server. Availability also depends on Apple’s Family Controls entitlement approval for distributed builds. Other widgets do not require Screen Time permission.

## Features not available yet

Purchases, subscription redemption and production Pro location history/place alerts are not open in this build. A preview or locked screen does not start a trial, subscription or charge. Plans has been removed from Love Bag.
